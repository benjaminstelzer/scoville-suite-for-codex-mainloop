# Layout and adaptation

Apply [Quality's ownership table](ui-quality.md#product-ownership-and-ui-guarantees).
Preserve intended grouping and responsive priorities through canonical components,
semantic relationships, valid reflow, persistent content/state and input behavior.

## Make relationships perceptible

Express meaningful groups, order and emphasis through the owning system.
Add containers or decoration only for a real relationship. Compare equivalent
meanings, behaviors and owner-backed variants; preserve deliberate differences.
Fix accidental drift at its owner within scope. Use Validation for geometry,
optical evidence and consistency coverage; a native value outside a numeric
scale alone is no defect.

## Adapt instead of merely shrinking

Derive adaptation from content, supported environments and owner-defined
transitions. Change flow, grouping, disclosure or density while preserving
meaning, required content/controls, status and recovery. Use one semantic flow
through canonical adaptation mechanisms where possible; clipping or hiding
required content does not solve overflow.

Give sequential tasks a clear order. Where frequent comparison or switching
benefits from simultaneous views, use the available space for related panes.
On smaller surfaces, preserve those relationships and needed working context
through a coherent sequence of views.

## Reassess composition after changes

After elements change, reassess the affected group's hierarchy, space and
transitions against its task. Adapt the local composition through its owner
when needed; preserve functions and settled decisions. Validation selects the
affected conditions and evidence; report a required wider redesign as a conflict.
